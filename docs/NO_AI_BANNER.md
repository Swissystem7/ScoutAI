| Badge | Meaning |
| --- | --- |

## Hard refusals under `NO_AI`

- **No video analysis** — no clip upload, no player-tracking from video, no CV/pose pipeline
- **No AI / ML inference** — Impact Score is hand weights (40/30/30), not a trained model
- **No account / SaaS** — browser lab only; no login, no ranking-as-a-service

The trap lab teaches why "face analysis" is not a metric **without implementing face or video analysis**.
